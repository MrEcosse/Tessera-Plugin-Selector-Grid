# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First version: every option of a selector on the tile at once, as a grid of keys. The options come from the entity:
  an input_select or select, a media player's inputs, or a remote's activities. A tap selects one, the one in use is lit,
  and when they do not all fit, arrows along the bottom page through them. Options can be left out. From one cell up to
  4x4.
